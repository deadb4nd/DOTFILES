Most bad glass quotes miss one of these six things:

1. Area, including cut-size waste
2. Glass type and thickness (toughened, low-iron, laminated)
3. Processing: edges, holes, notches. These are done before toughening, so a missed hole is a remake, not a tweak.
4. Hardware and finish
5. Labour, install and delivery
6. Your minimum charge
